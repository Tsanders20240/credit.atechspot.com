# Paid credit services — unavailable

Decision: paid credit repair, paid report review, paid consultation, report upload, representative bureau access, and power-of-attorney intake are **unavailable** on this website. The current public scope is free credit education and on-device planning tools. No payment link or fee is published.

Any future paid offering requires a separate written business decision, legal review of federal and each applicable state law, any required registration and security, approved contracts and cancellation notices, secure report-handling infrastructure, confirmed payment timing, and live customer-path verification. An educational label does not exempt an activity that is regulated as credit repair.

Clients can obtain their own free reports at https://www.annualcreditreport.com/ and dispute suspected factual errors themselves for free. The site does not collect or store reports or credentials. No exact score, 800 outcome, deletion, financing approval, or timeframe is guaranteed.
