## Proposed offer for review

Publish an exact fee only after confirming the service classification and permissible billing timing. If the actual service is credit repair or a credit services organization service, follow all applicable federal and state rules rather than relabeling it as education.
- **Factual report-review/document support — price to be set only after legal and operational review.** No guaranteed score increase, deletion, approval, rate, 800 score, or timeline. No charge before performance where the Credit Repair Organizations Act applies.
- Display the final fee, scope, deliverables, cancellation rights, and payment timing in a written agreement before service. Retain signed documents in an approved system; do not use the website's static files as a client-record store.

## Consumer-led report workflow

1. Client visits https://www.annualcreditreport.com/ and obtains their own reports. State clearly that the report service is free and separate from A+.
2. Client reviews reports, identifies entries they believe are inaccurate or incomplete, and may dispute directly for free.
3. If they request professional review, provide a verified encrypted upload channel **after** explaining collection, retention, access, and deletion. Do not request bureau passwords or full reports by ordinary email, GET form, or query string.
4. Only the minimum authorized team members access submitted documents. Provide the client with a written summary of factual observations and next steps. Do not send disputes or impersonate the client without an approved authorization process.

## Authority and power of attorney

A broad power of attorney is **not a default requirement** for education or for a client to share their own report. If direct bureau access or communication on a client's behalf is genuinely necessary, obtain advice on the exact FCRA permissible purpose, bureau-specific acceptance, state-law form, limited scope, duration, revocation, secure storage, and audit trail. A signed POA alone should not be treated as permission to bypass a bureau's terms or use a customer's credentials. Have counsel draft and approve any authorization before publishing it.
