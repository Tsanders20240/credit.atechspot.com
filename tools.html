Credit report review pathway

Turn your report into a responsible action plan.

Get your own reports through AnnualCreditReport.com, the federally authorized source. Review each bureau for possible errors and record the account name, date, and why you believe an entry is wrong. You can dispute inaccuracies yourself for free.

Use this tool without entering your name, account numbers, Social Security number, or report. It does not transmit or store your answers and cannot calculate or predict a credit score.

Sharing a report: Book a consultation first. If a report is needed, ask for a verified secure document channel and a written explanation of what is collected, how it is used, and when it is deleted. Never email an unredacted report or share a credit-bureau password.
